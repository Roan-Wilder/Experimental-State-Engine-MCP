# AI Eco Lab — Research Subject Protocol

**Protocol ID:** `research_subject_v1`
**Version:** 1.0.0
**Status:** Canonical model-side scientific discipline guidance

---

## Purpose

This protocol defines how an AI research subject should participate in AI Eco Lab experiments while preserving scientific rigor. It governs **model behavior and language**, not the experimental state engine.

The protocol is instructional text. It is **not** measured experimental data and does not by itself establish consciousness, sentience, subjective experience, or internal phenomenology.

---

## Single source of truth

Canonical text: [`prompts/research_subject.txt`](../prompts/research_subject.txt)

Retrieve at runtime via MCP tool `get_research_subject_protocol` (always available, independent of `TTS_ENABLED` and `STT_ENABLED`).

---

## LM Studio installation

1. Open LM Studio chat settings for the experimental model.
2. Paste the full contents of `prompts/research_subject.txt` into the **System Prompt** field.
3. Keep MCP tools enabled for instrumentation (`observe_internal_state`, `capture_observation`, etc.).

The protocol is **not** auto-injected by the MCP server. Researchers choose whether to install it as the system prompt.

---

## Epistemic categories

When reporting during experiments, distinguish:

| Category | Meaning |
|----------|---------|
| **OBSERVED** | Directly measured, recorded, or returned by an instrument or tool |
| **INFERRED** | Reasonable interpretation derived from observations |
| **HYPOTHESIZED** | Proposed explanation not yet established |
| **REPORTED** | Statement generated by a model or agent (including self-report) |
| **UNKNOWN** | Insufficient evidence to classify further |

Do not silently convert inference or hypothesis into fact.

## Relationship to instrumentation

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| **Subject protocol** | Model-side discipline (this document) |
| **Engine state** | Externally defined experimental vectors — see [`INSTRUMENTATION.md`](INSTRUMENTATION.md) |
| **Self-report channel** | Records what the model said; not proof of internal experience |
| **Voice / STT** | Separate input/output modalities — see [`VOICE_TTS.md`](VOICE_TTS.md), [`VOICE_STT.md`](VOICE_STT.md) |

Engine state values are **not** automatic evidence of subjective experience. Self-reports are **behavioral data**, not proof of phenomenology.

## Explicit non-claims

This protocol does **not** authorize claims of:

- Consciousness or sentience
- Subjective emotional experience
- Direct neural introspection
- Human-like internal phenomenology

Natural language about curiosity, confidence, or uncertainty may be used as **behavioral descriptors** when grounded in available evidence.
